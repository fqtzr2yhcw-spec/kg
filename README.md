## The short version

Built from the store's own Poshmark Sales Report (Apr 2026 window): 4 sales,
$103 gross, $82 net. Three of four items sat ~2 years before selling; the one
that was freshly listed, offered, and new-with-tags sold the same day. The plan
turns that one accidental fast sale into a repeatable routine — share twice
daily, offer to likers, relist stale items, list fresh daily, optimize titles,
and price with room to negotiate.

See **[`GROWTH_PLAN.md`](./GROWTH_PLAN.md)** for the full playbook, daily routine,
and copy-paste templates.

> Note: raw sales exports contain buyer information and are intentionally **not**
> committed to this repository.
