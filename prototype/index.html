Established

Both sides accept that our choices have prior neural and causal antecedents — deliberation doesn't happen in a vacuum.

Contested

Whether "freedom" requires the absence of causes, or merely the absence of coercion. The empirical facts are largely not in dispute — the disagreement is definitional.

What would resolve it

Agreeing on a shared definition of "free will" before arguing its existence. Name the definition and half the disagreement dissolves.
