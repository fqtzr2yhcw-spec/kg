Established

Both sides accept that our choices have prior neural and causal antecedents — deliberation doesn't happen in a vacuum.

Contested

Whether "freedom" requires the absence of causes, or merely the absence of coercion. The empirical facts are largely not in dispute — the disagreement is definitional.

What would resolve it

Agreeing on a shared definition of "free will" before arguing its existence. Name the definition and half the disagreement dissolves.

How the floor works · 1 / 4

Welcome to the floor.

perCVus — where what you say matters, and how you say it matters more.

It's a place to get closer to the truth by arguing well. Every floor is one motion, two sides, and a neutral referee — perCVus itself — that scores the reasoning, never the side.

You can be sharp, skeptical, even wrong. Being wrong is the raw material of working things out — it's never what gets punished.

Civility is a gate.

Not a scoreboard you can game. A brilliant point delivered as an insult earns nothing — the reasoning score is zeroed the moment it becomes an attack.

✗ “That's an idiotic take and you know it.” ✓ “That doesn't follow — here's the step it skips.”

Same disagreement, same force. Only one makes it onto the floor.

Strikes count from move one.

There's no warning shot — so this is your warning:

  • THROTTLE Your posts shrink to 256 characters. Earn your way back — 256 → 500 → 750 → full — with civil, well-scored arguments.
  • LOCKOUT Re-offend before you recover and you're locked out for 24 hours, noted openly in the thread.
  • REMOVED Three lockouts in a stretch and you're out — back only by petition, which we grant freely to anyone who asks in good faith.

We keep it small on purpose.

perCVus isn't for everyone — it's for people who'll contribute. That's a deliberate choice, and it's what keeps the floor worth showing up to.

Argue hard. Tread lightly.
