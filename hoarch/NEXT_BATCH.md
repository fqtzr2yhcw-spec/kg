# Next batch: ten more houses (after the Main Street shops)

Style references the user picked (photos shared in the session, not stored here). What each
one brings, and how it would print. Every house in the batch still gets its own everything:
no part, skin or ornament repeats from the first ten or from each other. Fish-scale ("U")
shingles stay retired; where a photo shows them, use another cut (diamond, sawtooth,
octagon, hexagon, staggered butt).

## 1. Turquoise gingerbread cottage (gold lace trim)
- Front-gable cottage whose gable is the show piece: a deep pierced bargeboard of scrolls
  and lace, a round medallion at the apex, a tall pointed-arch gable window under a sunburst
  hood with scroll ears.
- Full-width porch with a lacy "carpenter's lace" frieze: pierced spandrels that make each
  bay an elliptical arch between the posts; a pierced-panel railing.
- Ornate front door with a stained-glass light; scroll brackets at the eave returns.
- Colour split: body turquoise, all gingerbread gold, accents orange.
- Print: bargeboard, spandrel frieze and railing panels as flat face-up parts (one colour
  each); the medallion and hood as applied face-up trim.

## 2. Butter-yellow Queen Anne with a round-cornered porch
- A wraparound porch whose corner sweeps round in a curve, with a spindle-band frieze,
  turned posts, sawn brackets and a shingled skirt; a small pediment over the entry steps.
- Two-storey front-gable wing: gable panel with an applied sunburst, a shingle band
  between the storeys, bay windows with shingled aprons.
- Stick-style banding boards with rosettes, a recessed balcony in the side gable, iron
  cresting on a flat hip top, a tall corbelled chimney.
- Colour split: butter body, olive trim, burgundy accents.
- Print: the curved porch as arc segments (frieze and railing bent in plan, still printed
  upright); cresting face-up.

## 3. Coral tower house (crown-topped octagon)
- A central octagonal tower rising through the roof to a "crown": a ring of little gablets
  round a steep spire with a weathervane.
- Flanking front gables with fans; triangular window pediments with sunbursts over paired
  windows; an entry hood with a sunburst gable over glazed double doors.
- Coral body, teal trim bands at every floor.
- Print: the crown as its own part on the tower cap (gablets printed with the spire,
  upright); pediments face-up with the windows.

## 4. San Francisco twin row houses (Italianate pair)
- Two mirrored row houses as one model: slanted bays with Corinthian colonnettes, bracketed
  cornices with modillions and iron cresting, a columned portico with an arched pediment
  over arched double doors, segmental window hoods with keystones, tall stoops.
- A different colour scheme on each half (cream/gold and sage/green) in one kit.
- Must stay distinct from the Delancey: portico entry instead of a hooded door, Corinthian
  (not plain) colonnettes, cresting instead of a pediment parapet, mirrored pair.

## 5. Turquoise slate-roof Queen Anne with a loggia
- A round corner tower with a bell-cast conical roof, steep slate hips with a small
  bell-roofed dormer turret, a tall brick chimney.
- Wraparound porch on paired turned columns; a second-floor recessed loggia with columns
  and a railing over the porch; a pedimented porch gable with an ornament panel over the
  entry.
- Shingle zones in the upper storey, rock-faced stone foundation, a porte-cochere or side
  garage wing.
- Colour split: turquoise body, cream trim, navy accents.

## Fun extras seen in the photos (add-on parts)
- Picket fences and a gate with posts and finials (flat face-up strips, any length).
- Flower boxes under windows, porch rocking chairs, a bench, a bicycle, planters.
- Weathervanes and finials on the towers.
