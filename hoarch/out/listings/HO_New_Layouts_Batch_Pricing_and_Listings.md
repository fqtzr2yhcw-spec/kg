## Before you list

- **Photos first:** the real printed model in the main photo, then front 3/4, side, rear, close-ups and a
  scale shot with an HO figure or car. The renders in each zip can go last, captioned "digital render".
- **eBay:** Toys & Hobbies > Model Railroads & Trains > HO Scale > Buildings. Item specifics: Type: Building.
  Scale: HO (1:87). Material: Plastic. Assembly Required: Yes (kit) / No (built). Handmade: Yes. Country of
  Manufacture: United States.
- **Etsy:** Who made it: I did. What is it: A finished product. When was it made: Made to order.
- **Kits ship with the illustrated build guide** (the PDF in each zip).
- **"After" a real building is fine to say** (these are our own designs drawn from the real plan and
  massing); don't use the real building's name as if it were a licensed replica.
- **Before claiming the windows glow when lit,** test one with an LED inside. If it glows, add the
  bracketed lighting line.
