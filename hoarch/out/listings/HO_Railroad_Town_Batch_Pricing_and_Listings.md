## Before you list

- **Photos first:** the real printed model in the main photo, then front 3/4, side, rear, close-ups (the porch, the door, the roof detail) and a scale shot with an HO figure or car. The renders in each zip can go last, captioned "digital render".
- **eBay:** Toys & Hobbies > Model Railroads & Trains > HO Scale > Buildings, Tunnels & Bridges. Item specifics: Type: House. Scale: HO (1:87). Material: Plastic. Assembly Required: Yes (kit) / No (built). Handmade: Yes. Country of Manufacture: United States.
- **Etsy:** Who made it: I did. What is it: A finished product. When was it made: Made to order.
- **Kits ship with the illustrated build guide** (the PDF in each zip).
- **Paint notes for the built versions:** the church's buff brick bands, the firehouse's red soldier courses, the school's white corner boards, the letters on every sign and the clock dials are painted details (the renders are your guide); the other colours come straight off the printer.
- **Before claiming the windows glow when lit,** test one with an LED inside. If it glows, add the bracketed lighting line.
