## Before you list

- **Photos first:** the real printed model in the main photo, then front 3/4, side, rear, close-ups (the doors, the chutes, the roof detail) and a scale shot with an HO figure or car. The renders in each zip can go last, captioned "digital render".
- **eBay:** Toys & Hobbies > Model Railroads & Trains > HO Scale > Buildings, Tunnels & Bridges. Item specifics: Type: Building (Bridge or Tunnel Portal for those). Scale: HO (1:87). Material: Plastic. Assembly Required: Yes (kit) / No (built). Handmade: Yes. Country of Manufacture: United States.
- **Etsy:** Who made it: I did. What is it: A finished product. When was it made: Made to order.
- **Kits ship with the illustrated build guide** (the PDF in each zip).
- **Paint notes for the built versions:** the BLACKWATER, YARD OFFICE and date letters, the chute steel, the sand in the bin and the brick portals' stone trim (unless printed as the second colour) are painted details (the renders are your guide); the other colours come straight off the printer. For the portals, bridge and trestle a thin grey or creosote wash brings out the stonework and timber.
- **Before claiming the windows glow when lit,** test one with an LED inside. If it glows, add the bracketed lighting line.
