- **Lead with built & painted.** As with the earlier batches, the finished models are what earn $100+; the kit stays the cheaper option for modellers who like to build.
- **List first:** the Capistrano (a bell tower with a real hanging bell photographs beautifully), the Lindenwald (heart balusters, shutters and flower boxes read instantly) and the Kittredge (the airplane bungalow is a shape nobody else sells).
- **Target the layouts, not just the style.** The Capistrano and the Sandoval suit Santa Fe and Southwest layouts (put "santa fe layout" and "southwest house" in the tags). - **Christmas village crossover** works best for the Ashcombe (storybook Tudor) and the Lindenwald (Alpine chalet). Tag both and list them by mid-October.
- **Treat these as launch prices.** No watchers after two weeks: drop 10%. Sells in days: raise the next one.

## Before you list

- **Photos first:** the real printed model in the main photo, then front 3/4, side, rear, close-ups (the porch, the door, the roof detail) and a scale shot with an HO figure or car. The renders in each zip can go last, captioned "digital render".
- **eBay:** Toys & Hobbies > Model Railroads & Trains > HO Scale > Buildings, Tunnels & Bridges. Item specifics: Type: House. Scale: HO (1:87). Material: Plastic. Assembly Required: Yes (kit) / No (built). Handmade: Yes. Country of Manufacture: United States.
- **Etsy:** Who made it: I did. What is it: A finished product. When was it made: Made to order.
- **Kits ship with the illustrated build guide** (the PDF in each zip).
- **Paint notes for the built versions:** the Ashcombe's timbers (paint oak over the cream render) and diaper bricks, and the Pullman's stoop cheek walls, are painted details; the other colours come straight off the printer.
- **Before claiming the windows glow when lit,** test one with an LED inside. If it glows, add the bracketed lighting line.
