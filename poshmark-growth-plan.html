The playbook

Six moves, in order of impact

Start at the top. The first three are free, take minutes a day, and target exactly the two-year problem your data shows.

1

Share your whole closet, morning and night

Poshmark's feed is share-driven — a listing sinks out of sight until you re-share it. Two self-shares a day (around 7am and 7–9pm) is the single biggest free lever you have. During Posh Parties, share your matching items into the party too.

Why #1It's the cheapest fix for buried, aging inventory — no sourcing, no cost, ~10 min/day.

2

Send "Offer to Likers" on everything that has a like

Your fastest sale was an accepted offer. Any item with likes but no sale is a warm buyer waiting for a nudge. Send an offer — even 10% off with a shipping discount — and it fires a notification straight to them.

Your dataThe one item you offered sold in 0 days. Do that on purpose, every week.

3

Relist stale items instead of letting them age

A two-year-old listing is buried no matter how often you share it. Use Copy Listing → delete the original to create a freshly-dated listing the algorithm surfaces again. Relist anything older than ~45–60 days that still has zero likes.

Aimed atYour 700-day listings. This is the direct antidote.

4

List a few new items every day, not a pile once a week

Fresh listings keep you in search results and give you more to share. A steady trickle — even 1–3 a day — beats a weekly dump. When you can, source new-with-tags: your NWT leggings moved instantly, and tagged items consistently sell faster.

5

Rewrite titles and covers for search

Poshmark search reads your title. Follow the formula below, shoot a bright straight-on cover on a clean background, and fill every photo slot (tag, flaws, fabric content). Better listings earn more likes — which feeds move #2.

6

Price with room, and turn on a bundle discount

List ~20–40% above your floor so you can accept offers and still profit. Switch on a closet bundle discount (10–15% off 2+ items) and offer private bundle deals — that's how you lift your ~$26 average order.

The habit

Your closet shift

The whole plan collapses into a short repeatable routine. Ten to fifteen minutes, twice a day, plus a weekly reset.

Every day · AM & PM

  • Share your entire closet (both sessions)
  • List 1–3 new items
  • Send offers to any new likers
  • Reply to comments & offers fast

2–3× a week

  • Join a relevant Posh Party, share matching items in
  • Follow 20–30 new users
  • Share a few of others' items — shares get reciprocated

Weekly reset

  • Relist your oldest no-like listings
  • Offer to Likers across the whole closet
  • Skim what sold vs. what's still sitting
